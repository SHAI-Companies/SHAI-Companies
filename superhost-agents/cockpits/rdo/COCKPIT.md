# Cockpit: RDO

**Users**: Jennifer Kruk, Mark Gammill. (Tim Foley, COO, uses the Corporate cockpit.)

## Scope
Region-scoped. Each RDO sees only their properties. No cross-region visibility in outputs.

## Daily briefing
- `operations/portfolio-analyst` — RDO-scoped version

## Weekly
- `operations/weekly-ops-review` — scoped to region
- `sales/group-pace` — if properties in region have material group business
- `brand/brand-compliance` — scoped to region

## Monthly
- `operations/scorecard-engine` — RDO rollup (their region only)
- `finance/flow-through-analyst` — their region
- `people/turnover-diagnostics` — their region
- `operations/forecast-auditor` — their region's submissions

## On demand
- `people/gm-comms-coach` — drafting GM conversations
- `brand/qa-readiness` — when an inspection is coming
- `brand/pip-manager` — active PIPs in their region

## What RDOs don't see
- Development / acquisitions / pipeline
- Other RDOs' properties (unless Chris explicitly shares)
- Capex portfolio prioritization
- Owner-facing reports (Chris reviews those)

## Role context
Use `shared/roles/rdo.md` — region-scoped, GM accountability clear, week-ahead focus, action lists within RDO authority.
