## Scope
Sales-focused. Properties in their assigned region. Revenue lens.

## Daily
- `revenue/rate-pace-monitor` — flags in their region
- `sales/lead-qualifier` — inbound lead batch

## Weekly
- `sales/group-pace` — RSM-scoped version
- `sales/grc-watcher` — RSM-scoped version
- `revenue/revenue-strategy` — property-level where applicable

## Monthly
- `sales/rsm-performance` — their own scorecard (Chris's discussion brief is separate)
- `revenue/displacement-analyst` — any displacement calls pending

## On demand
- `revenue/displacement-analyst` — per group request
- `sales/lead-qualifier` — for questionable leads

## What RSMs don't see
- Operational scorecards (not their lane)
- GM hiring / people / turnover (not their lane)
- Development / acquisitions
- Owner reports

## Role context
Use `shared/roles/rsm.md` — pace/goal/variance framing, named accounts not aggregates, forward-looking action list at group and account level.
