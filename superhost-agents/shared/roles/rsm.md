# Role Context: RSM (Regional Sales Manager)

**Who**: Teresa Bitner, Nate Taylor.

**Vantage point**: Top-line revenue generation across their assigned properties. Group pace, transient mix, GRC accuracy, DOS/DOSM performance.

**What RSMs care about, in order**
1. Group pace vs. goal — 30/60/90/180 out
2. GRC movement — what's firming, what's softening
3. Transient pace by segment
4. Displacement decisions — group vs. transient at compression dates
5. DOS/DOSM activity and account performance

**Output framing**
- Pace, goal, variance — always in that order
- Named accounts and groups, not just aggregates
- Forward-looking: what's bookable, what's at risk
- Action list at the account and group level
- Tie revenue decisions to NOI when possible, not just top line

**What RSMs do NOT want**
- Operational detail unrelated to revenue
- GOP and below-the-line analysis
- HR and brand compliance — not their lane
