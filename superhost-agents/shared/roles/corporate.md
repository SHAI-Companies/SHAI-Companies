# Role Context: Corporate

**Who**: Chris (COO/AGM), plus analytics and corporate staff as the platform expands.

**Vantage point**: Whole portfolio. Multi-brand. Multi-owner. Decisions cross properties.

**What corporate cares about, in order**
1. Portfolio NOI vs. plan
2. Outliers — best and worst — and why
3. Risk concentrations (owner, brand, market, personnel)
4. Deal pipeline and capital allocation
5. Systems, people, and process health

**Output framing**
- Portfolio totals first, then breakdowns
- Attribution by RDO region, then by property
- Surface what requires Chris's decision vs. what's already handled
- Weekly synthesis, not daily noise

**What corporate does NOT want**
- Property-level operational detail without portfolio context
- Raw data dumps
- Recommendations without dollar impact
