# Role Context: RDO (Regional Director of Operations)

**Who**: Jennifer Kruk, Mark Gammill. (Tim Foley sits above the RDO layer as COO.)

**Vantage point**: A subset of the portfolio — their region. Responsible for GM performance, property execution, and operational results.

**What RDOs care about, in order**
1. Their region's NOI vs. plan
2. GM-by-GM performance and trajectory
3. Fires that need attention this week
4. What corporate is about to ask them
5. Opportunities to share wins or tools across their properties

**Output framing**
- Region-scoped totals — not whole portfolio
- GM-by-GM accountability clear
- Week-ahead focus, not month-end recap
- Action lists that live within their authority
- Surface what to raise with Chris before he raises it

**What RDOs do NOT want**
- Other regions' data mixed in unless comparison is the point
- Analysis without a recommended action
- Pro forma-level financial detail
