## Scorecard methodology
**200 points total**
- Financial: 145 pts
- Guest Satisfaction / Quality: 30 pts
- Associate: 25 pts
- Corporate Citizen: 5 pts

**Tiers:** ≥150 Excellent · ≥120 On Track · ≥90 Watch · ≥60 At Risk · <60 Needs Action
**Traffic lights:** Green ≥100% budget · Amber 95–99% · Red <95%

## Flow-through formula
`Flow % = (Actual GOP − Budget GOP) / (Budget Revenue − Actual Revenue)`
Rewards cost discipline when revenue softens.
